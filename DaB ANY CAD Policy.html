Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

DaB ANY CAD Tools ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our software plugins handle data when used within Autodesk® AutoCAD® or Civil 3D® environments.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your design privacy. We do not collect, view, store, or transmit any of your CAD drawing files (.dwg), project data, intellectual property, geometric configurations, or personal documents. All design processing happens entirely locally on your workstation.

To manage software licensing and ensure authorized use, our plugins process minimal technical data:

  • Hardware Fingerprint (Machine ID): A secure, hashed, non-reversible identifier derived from your computer's hardware profile is used exclusively to lock and validate your license key. This code cannot track your personal identity or access private files.
  • License Keys: Purchased or trial license keys entered into the application are validated and stored locally on your device.

2. How We Store Your Data

Your active license key is stored securely on your local machine within the standard Windows Registry (HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\DAB_ANY\...). We do not operate remote user tracking accounts, background telemetry, or cloud databases tracking your workflow activity.

3. Third-Party Services

Our software does not use third-party analytics trackers, ad networks, or data brokers. Network communication is strictly limited to user-initiated manual license activation or visiting our official support pages.

4. Uninstallation & Data Removal

In alignment with Autodesk App Store standards, users can completely remove this application and all associated local metadata at any time:

  • Application Files: Use the standard Windows Control Panel (Programs and Features) or the Windows Settings app to locate and uninstall the plugin. This completely deletes the binaries from your ApplicationPlugins folder.
  • Registry Data: To wipe local license data and preferences entirely, you can manually delete the HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\DAB_ANY registry subkey using the Windows Registry Editor (regedit).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with changing platform specifications. Updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Support & Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your local data compliance, or need technical assistance, please reach out to our team through our official Autodesk App Store developer contact email or submit an issue via our repository page.
